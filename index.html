Frequently Asked Questions

How do I purchase and download books?

Simply browse our store, select a book, click "Buy Now", and proceed to checkout with Razorpay. Your book will be instantly available in PDF and EPUB formats for download and reading in your library.

Can I read books online or do I have to download them?

You can do both! Our in-browser reader lets you preview and read books instantly. You can also download them in PDF or EPUB format for offline reading on any device.

What formats are available?

All books are available in PDF and EPUB formats. PDF is ideal for reading on computers and tablets, while EPUB is optimized for e-readers and mobile devices.

Do I have lifetime access?

Yes! Once you purchase a book, you own it forever. You can download and read it as many times as you want, on any device, with no expiration date.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major payment methods through Razorpay: credit/debit cards, UPI, net banking, digital wallets, and more. Payments are secure and encrypted.

Are there any hidden fees?

No. The price you see is the final price. No hidden charges or subscription fees. You pay once and own the book forever.
